Mmmap puts every restaurant in the MICHELIN Guide on a single map — from three-star temples of gastronomy to Bib Gourmand hidden gems.

Features:
• Browse all MICHELIN Guide restaurants on an offline-first map (data bundled, no account required)
• Filter by distinction (★★★ / ★★ / ★ / Bib Gourmand / Selected)
• Filter by cuisine and price range
• Tap any pin for details: phone, website, cuisine, and facilities from the MICHELIN dataset
• Near Me — instantly see top restaurants sorted by your distance
• Direct deep-link to each restaurant's official MICHELIN Guide page
• Mark restaurants as visited; export and import your visited list

Privacy: no tracking, no analytics, no account required.

Note: dataset updates are fetched from GitHub when available.
